Equity Trading

Equity trading involves buying and selling stocks or equity shares of publicly listed companies. During equity trading, investors analyze stock valuations and market trends to make timely buy and sell decisions.

Forex Trading

Forex (Foreign Exchange) trading entails the exchange of currencies with each other. This market operates 24 hours a day and involves trading currencies from around the world. Forex traders analyze currency pairs and make trading decisions based on market volatility.

Commodity Trading

Commodity trading involves the buying and selling of futures contracts for raw materials and physical goods such as gold, silver, crude oil, grains, and metals. This market is influenced by global demand, supply, and geopolitical factors. Traders analyze commodity prices and market trends to take trading positions.

Options Trading

Options trading is a derivative instrument where investors buy a contract that gives them the right to buy or sell a specific asset at a specific price within a specific period. Options traders use trading strategies based on market volatility, price movements, and time decay.

Futures Trading

Futures trading involves trading agreements where investors agree to buy or sell a specific asset at a specific price at a future time. Futures contracts can be based on commodities, currencies, stocks, or interest rates. Traders take trading positions based on market expectations and risk management.

Algorithmic Trading

Algorithmic trading is an automated trading process where predefined rules and algorithms are used to make trading decisions. It utilizes high-speed computers and advanced mathematical models to identify trading opportunities and automate trade executions. Investors use market data and signals to execute trades instantaneously.
